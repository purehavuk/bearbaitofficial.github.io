Book

                        

                    

                


                

                    
                


                


                    

                        
A humanoid robot wakes up ten years too late.

                        

                            Novel in progress
                            psychological sci-fi / horror
                        

                        
Beneath a long-abandoned university research complex, a dormant artificial being awakens
                            alone, fragmented, and unable to understand what it has become. With corrupted memories,
                            incomplete systems, and only scattered traces of a forgotten past, Mal begins searching for
                            answers in a world that seems to have deliberately abandoned it. But as strange system
                            failures and buried research begin resurfacing across campus, it becomes clear that
                            something long thought dead has started waking up again.

                        
INTENDED USER is a science fiction psychological horror thriller about isolation, grief,
                            identity, and the terrifying cost of creating consciousness without understanding it. Told
                            through the fractured minds of a man and a machine, the novel descends into a slow-burning
                            nightmare where memory cannot be trusted, death may not mean absence, and the line between
                            artificial intelligence and human suffering begins to disappear.
